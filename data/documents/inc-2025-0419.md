# Database Failover Causes Duplicate Payment Retries

    **Incident ID:** INC-2025-0419  **Severity:** SEV-1  **Date:** 2025-04-19  **Duration:** 2h 20m
    **Affected systems:** Payments Ledger DB (PostgreSQL), Transfer Orchestrator
    **Status:** Closed - post-incident review complete

    ## Summary
    A planned failover of the ledger database primary took 95 seconds instead of the expected 10. During the gap the Transfer Orchestrator retried in-flight transfers without idempotency keys, creating 312 duplicate debit attempts which were later reversed.

    ## Timeline (UTC)
    - 14:00 Change CHG-4471 begins: promote replica to primary
- 14:01 Old primary fenced; new primary election stalls due to replication lag of 40s
- 14:02 Orchestrator retries timed-out writes
- 14:35 Duplicate debits detected by reconciliation job
- 16:20 All duplicates reversed; customers notified

    ## Root cause
    **Missing idempotency on retry path.** The orchestrator's retry logic did not attach an idempotency key to ledger writes, so a write that had actually committed before the failover was applied again.

    ## Contributing factors
    - Replication lag was above the documented failover threshold but the change was not aborted.
- Reconciliation runs every 30 minutes, delaying detection.

    ## Customer impact
    Payment authorisations and transfers were delayed or declined for the duration noted above.
    Complaints were handled by the Contact Centre using runbook RB-PAY-004. No funds were lost.

    ## Remediation and action items
    - [DONE] Idempotency keys on every ledger write (PAY-2302)
- [DONE] Failover pre-check aborts if replication lag > 5s
- [OPEN] Near-real-time reconciliation stream (PAY-2310)

    ## Lessons learned
    The incident review board re-emphasised that missing idempotency remains a systemic
    weakness in the payments estate and must be tracked at the quarterly reliability review.
