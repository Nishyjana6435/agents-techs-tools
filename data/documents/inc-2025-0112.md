# Payment Gateway Timeouts During Morning Peak

    **Incident ID:** INC-2025-0112  **Severity:** SEV-2  **Date:** 2025-01-12  **Duration:** 1h 42m
    **Affected systems:** PayCore Gateway, Card Authorisation Service
    **Status:** Closed - post-incident review complete

    ## Summary
    Between 07:55 and 09:37 approximately 18% of card authorisations timed out. The PayCore gateway exhausted its outbound HTTP connection pool to the acquirer network after a latency spike at the acquirer.

    ## Timeline (UTC)
    - 07:55 Latency to acquirer rises from 120ms to 2.1s
- 08:03 Connection pool (max 200) saturated; queueing begins
- 08:10 PagerDuty alert PAY-LATENCY-P95 fires
- 08:40 On-call increases pool size and enables circuit breaker manually
- 09:37 Acquirer latency recovers; error rate returns to baseline

    ## Root cause
    **Connection pool exhaustion.** The gateway used a fixed pool of 200 connections with a 30s timeout and no circuit breaker. When the acquirer slowed down, every connection was held for the full timeout, starving new requests.

    ## Contributing factors
    - Circuit breaker existed in code but was disabled by feature flag `pay.cb.enabled=false` since the Q4 2024 migration.
- No load-shedding at the API edge.

    ## Customer impact
    Payment authorisations and transfers were delayed or declined for the duration noted above.
    Complaints were handled by the Contact Centre using runbook RB-PAY-004. No funds were lost.

    ## Remediation and action items
    - [DONE] Enable circuit breaker with 50% error-rate trip threshold
- [DONE] Reduce acquirer timeout to 8s
- [OPEN] Adaptive connection pool sizing (PAY-2211)

    ## Lessons learned
    The incident review board re-emphasised that connection pool exhaustion remains a systemic
    weakness in the payments estate and must be tracked at the quarterly reliability review.
